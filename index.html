05 How it works

1 A laser pulse leaves the scanner and bounces off a surface; the travel time gives the range.

2 A mirror spins around a horizontal axis and sweeps the beam through a vertical plane (about 300 degrees, the bottom cone is the blind spot).

3 The head turns slowly around the vertical axis, 180 degrees in total. Each mirror turn paints two opposite columns.

4 Every range plus the two angles becomes one 3D point. Together they form the 360 degree panorama and the point cloud.

5 Pillars and machines cast shadows: what the scanner cannot see stays empty. Moving robots leave ghosts.
